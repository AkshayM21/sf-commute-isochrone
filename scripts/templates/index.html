How it works

The map shows door-to-door travel time by walking + public transit only (Muni / BART / Caltrain) — no driving or biking — on real weekday schedules, . Each area is colored by how long it takes to reach your workplace; hover or tap any area for the route behind the number.

Best-case assumes perfect timing — you leave to the second and never wait. Realistic is a Monte-Carlo "committed-plan" estimate: you commit to a departure and a first line, then service noise (a late vehicle, a missed transfer) adds a typical wait.

Walking is hill-aware — uphill is slower than downhill (Tobler walking speeds over an elevation model).

About

SF Commute Explorer maps where in San Francisco you can live and still reach a workplace you choose within a reachable transit commute.

Built by Akshay Manglik.

Under the hood it runs a self-built RAPTOR transit router and a hill-aware walk graph over OpenStreetMap + elevation data — no heavyweight routing backend, so the whole thing serves the full city in a fraction of a second.
